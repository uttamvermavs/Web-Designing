Tiger Shroff Car Firing Case: Juhu Attack, Bishnoi Gang Link, and Multi-State Arrests Explored

For More Info:

On the evening of October 18, 2026, motorcycle-borne attackers fired five to seven rounds at Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff's car in Mumbai's Juhu area. The incident occurred near Pushpa Park around 7 PM as Shroff was leaving a local football ground. While the actor miraculously escaped with minor injuries from shattering glass, his driver Firdaus and a bystander were injured in the shooting. Following the attack, an overseas-based gangster identified as Anil Pandit claimed responsibility via social media on behalf of the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, warning anyone collaborating with actor Salman Khan

Crackdown and Arrests: Multiple Suspects Apprehended Across States

Following a multi-state operation, law enforcement agencies have arrested multiple suspects linked to the conspiracy. Three primary suspects—identified as Phool Singh, Hrithik Kumar, and Rehan—were intercepted by the Rajasthan Police at the Kota railway station while attempting to flee toward Nepal. Additionally, the Delhi Police Special Cell arrested four individuals, including two women, who allegedly transported the firearms used in the operation under the directions of Anil Pandit. The arrested individuals have been handed over to the Mumbai Police Anti-Extortion Cell and remain in custody as the hunt for the missing weapons continues

Widening Probe: Gangster Named Wanted as Investigation Exposes Larger Hitlist

The Mumbai Police have officially named gangster Anil Pandit as a wanted accused in the case, confirming to the court that the shooters acted strictly on his instructions. Investigating teams revealed that the assailants initially targeted Salman Khan's residence but shifted focus to Tiger Shroff due to strict security deployment at Khan's house. The ongoing investigation has also uncovered alarming details suggesting that the suspects had conducted reeces of other high-profile Bollywood targets, including Ranbir Kapoor's residence. Maharashtra government authorities have ordered a stringent crackdown against the syndicate and reinforced celebrity security protocols across Mumbai.
